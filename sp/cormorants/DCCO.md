Common diving bird in most parts of inland North America. It can often be seen drying itself on land from a perch due to having reduced oil on its feathers, an adaptation that improves diving ability at the expense of dryness.

<hr class="thin-line">

**Appearance:** large, black-ish bird that appears lighter in bright lighting. The face is bright orange, with teal eyes. Breeding adults may have tufts of white feathers on their heads.

**Voice:** mostly silent. *May make grunting noises.*

**Behaviour:** swims and dives for fish and other aquatic prey. When not in water, can be found on land and in trees, often drying wings in the air.

**Known whereabouts:** most water bodies with sufficient food resources, such as ponds, lakes, and marshes.

**First seen in:** Edmonton
