Fairly abundant blackbird. Noisy and charismatic, grackles often appear to be silly and mildly menacing. They may be considered an agricultural pest at times due to damaging crops ~~but they have great defense lawyers and have won every single court case against them so far~~. 

<hr class="thin-line">

**Appearance:** Shiny. Black. Iridescent. Juveniles and to a lesser extent females are duller, being more brownish.

**Voice:** song is a metallic, harsh scraping sound. Calls are typically "chuk" with variations such as trilling.

**Behaviour:** can be found in flocks with its own species or with other icterids. Noisily forages for food anywhere.

**Known whereabouts:** near water, but not in the water.

**First seen in:** Edmonton
